Common questions

What does Smack do?

You film a golf swing, or import one from your camera roll. Smack finds the phases of the swing — address, takeaway, top of backswing, impact, follow-through and the points between — measures your body position at each one, and tells you which measurements are in a good range and which aren't. Every swing is saved so you can watch your numbers change over time.

How should I film a swing?

Three things matter most:

  • Film in slow motion. A normal-speed video works, but slow motion is much more accurate.
  • Film one swing per video, and trim it to just the swing.
  • Keep your whole body in frame, centered, from either face-on or down-the-line.

Why does a measurement say it couldn't be measured?

A measurement needs to see the body parts it depends on. If your feet are cut off, the lighting is low, or your clothing blends into the background, that measurement is skipped rather than guessed at. Skipped measurements don't count against your score. Refilming with your whole body in frame usually fixes it.

Does Smack analyze putting?

No. Smack isn't built for putting strokes.

Where are my swings stored?

On your iPhone. Swings aren't uploaded anywhere, so deleting the app deletes them. A swing you delete in the app sits in Recently Deleted for 30 days, where you can restore it, and is then removed for good.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also reach this from Smack's Settings tab under Manage Subscription. Canceling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the period you've paid for ends.

I subscribed before, but Smack is locked

Open Smack's Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I turn reminders off?

Smack sends one reminder if you haven't analyzed a swing in a week. To stop them, open your iPhone's Settings, find Smack, tap Notifications, and turn them off.
