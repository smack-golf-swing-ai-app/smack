Privacy policy

Smack: Golf Swing AI Analyzer · Effective October 4, 2026

Smack does not collect your personal information. Your videos and your swing results stay on your iPhone. There is no account to create, no server storing your swings, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

This policy explains that in full, including the few situations where information does reach us — all of which start with you choosing to send it.

What stays on your device

All of the following is created and stored on your iPhone, and never transmitted to us:

  • The swing videos you record or import, and the frames taken from them
  • The measurements, scores, phase images and history for every swing you save
  • The name you enter and the answers you give during setup
  • Your app settings

The analysis runs entirely on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device vision framework. No video or image is uploaded for processing.

Deleting the app removes all of it. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

Camera and photo library

Smack asks for camera access when you record a swing, and for a video when you import one. Those permissions are used only to get a swing into the app for analysis on your device. When you import, iOS hands Smack only the video you pick — the rest of your library is never visible to the app.

When you email us

If you use Contact Us, Report a Bug, or Report an Issue With This Swing, your email program opens with a message you can read and edit before sending. If you send it, we receive what that message contains:

  • Your email address and whatever you write
  • The app version and your iOS version
  • For a swing issue report: that swing's images and its measurements

We use those reports only to answer you and to fix the problem, and we keep them no longer than we need to for that. We don't add you to a mailing list. If you'd like your email and its attachments deleted after we've finished helping, just ask.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. Payment happens in Apple's system, and we never see or handle your payment details, card number, or Apple Account credentials. The app asks Apple whether your subscription is active; Apple answers yes or no. Apple provides us with aggregate sales reports that do not identify individual customers. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Smack schedules a reminder on your device when you haven't analyzed a swing in a week. These are scheduled by the app on your iPhone. No notification is sent from a server, and no information is transmitted to create one.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

Smack contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party tracking tools. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Smack is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to delete; if you believe a child has emailed us, contact us and we'll remove the message.

Your choices

  • Delete a swing in the app, or delete the app, to remove your data from your device.
  • Change camera, photo and notification permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Email us to ask that a support message you sent be deleted.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
